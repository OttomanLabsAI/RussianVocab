## Self-check (the default)

Reveal the card and the question is **Did you get it right?** with two buttons: **Incorrect** and **Correct**. Nothing else on the card. Underneath, Correct is "Good" and Incorrect is "Again", so the spaced-repetition scheduling is unchanged — a word you miss comes back in a minute, a word you know moves out as before. Keys: space reveals, 1 Incorrect, 2 Correct.

## Multiple choice

A checkbox at the top of the review settings: **Multiple choice · pick from 5**. Tick it and every card shows the word with five answers underneath — the right one plus four others. The others come from your own file first (same part of speech, so a noun is hidden among nouns), then the rest of the file, then common dictionary words, never two that read the same. Your pick decides it: the right answer fills in, a wrong pick turns red with the correct one shown, and **Next** moves on. It works both ways round — with Direction set to EN → RU the five choices are Russian words. Keys 1–5 pick, space goes to the next card.
