Good news first: that screenshot says "◀ TestFlight" in the corner — the app is installed through TestFlight, so the whole pipeline works now.

The two problems are one problem. On iPhone the keyboard sits over the tab bar, and the sign-in form gave you no way to put the keyboard away: tapping outside a field doesn't dismiss it in SwiftUI, and the email keyboard's return key did nothing. So once you tapped into Email, the tabs were hidden behind the keyboard and the page looked stuck.

## v1.16

- Every screen with a text field — Account, Search, Sets, Lists, Teaching, a student's page, and the first-run sign-in — now shows a **Done** button above the keyboard, and dragging the page down dismisses it too.
- The return key does the right thing: **Next** on Email moves to Password, **Go** on Password signs you in, **Search** in Search puts the keyboard away.
- Signing in also dismisses the keyboard, so the tab bar is back the moment you're in.

## Get it onto the phone

```
cd ~/Vocab-Folio && git pull
cd ios && xcodegen generate
```

Open the project, *Any iOS Device (arm64)* → Product → Archive → Distribute App → TestFlight Internal Only. App version is 1.16, so App Store Connect accepts it alongside the 1.14 build; the TestFlight app on your phone shows **Update** once it has processed.
